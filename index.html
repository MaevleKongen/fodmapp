✦ Explore features

Camera, barcode and chat

Select a feature to see what it can do for you.

Ask a question and get an answer in seconds – tailored to you.

💡 Suggestions for you

The chat suggests questions based on your scans and meals, such as low-FODMAP alternatives to something you've scanned or a recipe for something you've eaten.

🙋 Knows you from the start

The answers you give when you start using the app are used as background in the chat, so you don't have to repeat yourself. You can change them anytime.

🥦 Evaluate any food

Type a food, a dish or a product name and get a FODMAP assessment with an explanation, portion advice and low-FODMAP alternatives.

🍽️ Analyse restaurant dishes

Describe a restaurant dish, and the AI assesses each ingredient, explains the FODMAP content and suggests how to order a low-FODMAP version.

📅 Weekly meal plans

Ask for a full low-FODMAP weekly menu with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks – the AI creates a varied and balanced plan with recipes.

🛒 Shopping lists

After your meal plan, ask for a shopping list. The AI generates one based on the menu so you always have the right ingredients at home.

❓ FODMAP questions

Ask what FODMAP is, which types exist (fructans, galactans, polyols, fructose, lactose), how the diet works, or anything else related to FODMAP.

🧘 Stress management

Ask the AI for relaxation techniques, breathing exercises and practical tips for handling stress in everyday life.

🕒 Deleted after 24 hours

Messages stay in the chat for 24 hours and are then deleted automatically.

Point the camera at the dish, the menu, the buffet or the fridge, and see the FODMAP level of every ingredient, dish and product.

🍲 Dish

Snap your plate. The food scanner recognizes the ingredients, estimates the amounts and shows which are low, moderate and high in FODMAP, with swap ideas for a lower level.

📋 Restaurant menu

Snap a menu in any language. The dishes appear in your language with their FODMAP level, and under “To the waiter” you get a ready-made note in the menu's language to show.

🧊 Fridge

Snap your fridge. FODMAPP finds the foods, and you can add anything the AI missed. Then you get recipes made only from the low-FODMAP foods, scaled to the number of servings.

🔢 Levels and FODMAP groups

Every scan shows the level with a color and three bars: low, moderate or high. You also see the amount and which FODMAP group sets the level, such as fructans.

🥗 Hot and cold buffets

Take one photo of the whole buffet table. The AI finds the dishes and shows which are low, moderate and high in FODMAP, so you get the full picture fast.

🍽️ Log what you ate

Tap “Log as eaten” once you've eaten what you scanned. You choose the portion afterwards, and the meal goes straight into your diary.

🗂️ My scans

All your scans are gathered in one place, with search. Filter by dish, menu, buffet, fridge or barcode, and open an earlier result again.

Point the camera at any product's barcode and get a full FODMAP assessment in seconds.

🌍 Over 4.4 million products

The barcode scanner taps into Open Food Facts – one of the world's largest open food databases with over 4.4 million products from countries around the globe. Whether you're at home or travelling abroad, chances are the product is already in the database.

📊 AI-powered ingredient analysis

When you scan the barcode, the app fetches the ingredient list on the pack and assesses every ingredient, with serving size and lower-FODMAP alternatives.

✅ Shopping made easier

Use the scanner before putting anything in your trolley. It works with packaged products from any country – perfect when travelling or shopping at international stores.

✦ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the low-FODMAP diet?

▶

A research-based dietary approach to reduce digestive issues in people with IBS, by limiting certain hard-to-digest carbohydrates.

How do you handle privacy?

▶

Your AI chat conversations are deleted automatically after 24 hours, and the food and symptom entries you record are stored on your account so that you have a history – you can delete them in the app at any time. Photos you take for analysis are stored only temporarily with us while the AI analyses them, and are deleted automatically within one hour. They are not saved in your scan history. Text and images are processed by the AI models Gemini from Google and Claude from Anthropic on the basis of your explicit consent. We never sell your data and never use it to target advertising.

Is the app available on iOS and Android?

▶

Yes, FODMAPP is available for both iPhone and Android phones.

How accurate is the AI?

▶

FODMAPP uses advanced AI that assesses FODMAP content based on established research. AI can make mistakes and should be used alongside professional advice, not instead of it.

Is the app free?

▶

Every new user gets a free 14-day trial with full access to all features. After the trial, continued access to the AI features requires a FODMAPP Plus subscription, available as a monthly or yearly plan. Current prices are always shown in the app before you buy.

What languages does the app support?

▶

FODMAPP is available in 23 languages including English, Norwegian, Swedish, Danish, Finnish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Indonesian, Lithuanian, Thai, and Arabic.
