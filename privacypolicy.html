Privacy Policy

Here we explain how we process personal data when you use the FODMAPP app.

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Version 2.1

1. About this privacy policy

This privacy policy explains how Telemark Software Solutions AS processes personal data when you use the FODMAPP app and our website. It applies to everyone who uses FODMAPP.

Telemark Software Solutions AS is the data controller for the processing described here. You can contact us at post@fodmapp.com.

FODMAPP is built on Google Firebase for authentication, storage and operations, and uses AI models from two providers for the AI features: Gemini from Google (via Google Cloud Vertex AI) and Claude from Anthropic. Because the app deals with food, digestion and symptoms, we process health data, and we therefore explain in particular detail below what happens to that information.

2. In brief

  • We process information about your diet, digestion and symptoms. This is health data, and we process it on the basis of your explicit consent, with one exception for emails to us, comments on ratings and AI answers you report; see section 4.
  • Your AI chat conversations are deleted automatically after 24 hours. The food and symptom entries you record are stored on your account so that you have a history, and you can delete them in the app at any time.
  • Text and images you send to the AI features are transferred to the AI models of Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude) for processing. Which provider is used depends on the feature and the app version; see section 6. We do not use your data to train our own AI models, and the providers do not use it to train theirs.
  • Photos you take for analysis are stored only temporarily. They are uploaded to our cloud storage, sent from there to the AI provider for the analysis and deleted automatically within one hour.
  • We send you the service messages needed to run your account. Our newsletter is sent only if you switch it on in the app’s settings, and you can switch it off again at any time.
  • We never sell your personal data, and we do not use it to target advertising or for third-party profiling.
  • You can request access, correction and deletion at any time, and withdraw your consent in the app.

3. What personal data we collect

a) Account data

  • Email address, and first and last name if you provide them
  • Password, stored in encrypted (hashed) form – or your login method if you use Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, in which case we receive your name and email address from them, and your telephone number and profile picture where the provider supplies them
  • Language setting, app version and country/region
  • Record of the consents you have given: which version of the terms and privacy policy you accepted and when, whether you have consented to the processing of health data, when and to which version of the consent text, whether you have consented to marketing, and your acceptance of the age requirement (at least 18 years), recorded when you accept the terms

b) Subscription data

  • Subscription status and type, trial period status, start and renewal dates
  • A pseudonymous subscriber ID with RevenueCat, and the purchase history reported by the app store
  • A record of when your subscription has renewed: month, plan (monthly or yearly), country and store (App Store or Google Play). It is linked to your user ID, not to your name or email address, and contains no health data; see section 10
  • On iPhone and iPad, the app sends an attribution token from Apple to RevenueCat. If you downloaded the app after tapping one of our ads in the App Store (Apple Ads), RevenueCat then receives from Apple the campaign, ad group and keyword of that ad, and stores this together with your pseudonymous subscriber ID. We use it only to see which of our ads lead to downloads and subscriptions. The token contains no health data, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites
  • We never receive or store your card details. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google

c) Health-related data

  • The messages you write in the AI chat, including anything you tell us about digestion, symptoms, reactions to food, diagnoses or medication. Chat messages are deleted automatically after 24 hours; see section 10
  • The entries you record in the app: meals, symptom and pain logs, bowel movements (Bristol scale), sleep, stress, menstruation, medication and notes
  • The answers you give when you set up the app (“Your setup”): how your stomach usually is, which complaints you have, foods you yourself suspect, and what you have tried before
  • The AI’s analyses, FODMAP assessments and recommendations linked to your account
  • The history of the above, which is stored on your account until you delete it, withdraw your consent or delete your account

d) Images

  • Photographs you take or upload for analysis – food, dishes, menus, ingredient lists, barcodes or the contents of your fridge
  • The image is uploaded to our cloud storage (Google Firebase) and sent from there to the AI provider for the analysis. It is deleted automatically within one hour and is not stored with your scan history

e) Technical and usage data

  • Device model, operating system, app version and language
  • A verification token from Firebase App Check, which confirms that requests to our server functions come from a genuine FODMAPP app (via Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple’s DeviceCheck on iOS). It is used only to prevent misuse
  • Crash and error reports via Firebase Crashlytics, and performance data such as app start-up and network timings via Firebase Performance Monitoring
  • Usage statistics via Google Analytics for Firebase, such as which screens and features are used – including which onboarding steps you complete
  • Simple counters kept on your account, such as how many times you have opened the app and how many chat messages and camera analyses you have used
  • Usage events are linked to an app-instance identifier and to your user ID, and on Android the analytics service may also collect the device’s advertising ID. This data is therefore personal data. We use it only to understand and improve the app – never for advertising, and we do not share it with advertising networks; see section 14

f) Correspondence and feedback

  • Emails and support enquiries you send us, and our replies
  • Bug reports and wishes you send in the app. They contain your user ID, the text you write and technical information: app version, device model, operating system and language. We do not attach your health data, but you decide what you write, and we encourage you not to include more health information than necessary. We also record when you sent the feedback, that you consented, and whether you ticked the box for automatic translation. If you did, we also store the Norwegian translation; see section 6
  • An image, such as a screenshot, if you choose to attach one. We remove location, time and other information about the image before it is stored; we keep only what is needed to display it correctly, such as its orientation. A screenshot shows what was on your screen, including any health information, so check it before you send it. Only you and we have access to it
  • The email address on your account, only if you tick the box allowing us to contact you about the matter
  • Ratings you give in the app: the number of stars, any comment, your user ID, the app version, whether you use iOS or Android, the time and where in the app you gave the rating. We also record when we have asked you for a rating and what you answered, and when the app has requested the store’s own rating dialogue. These records stay on your account for as long as you have it, like the usage counters
  • AI answers you report in the chat: the answer itself, your user ID and the time. The rest of the conversation is not included

g) Location

  • If you use the toilet finder, your approximate location is sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service to look up nearby toilets. We do not store your location, and it is not linked to your account

4. Health data and your explicit consent

Information about digestion, symptoms and reactions to food is classified as data concerning health, which is a special category of personal data under Article 9 of the GDPR. Such data may only be processed where a specific condition is met.

We process your health data on the basis of your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a), together with Article 6(1)(a), with one exception for emails, ratings and reported AI answers (see below). For the AI features, you give this consent in a separate dialogue in the app before they are made available to you, and it covers the storage of your conversations and entries and the transfer of the content to our AI providers, Google and Anthropic, for processing (see section 6). If we change which providers receive the content, we will ask for your consent again in the app before you can use the AI features again.

For feedback (bug reports and wishes), which you send under Profile → Report a problem or send a wish, you give a separate consent, distinct from the AI dialogue, when you send the feedback. The line above the Send button reads: “By sending, you agree that we process what you write and the image you attach, including any health information, to follow up on your feedback.” The consent for feedback therefore covers both the text and any image; see section 3(f).

The exception applies to health data you yourself write in an email to us or in a comment on a rating you give in the app, and to health data contained in an AI answer you report in the chat. We use it only to reply to you, fix errors and improve the app, and it is deleted as described in section 10.

The consents are voluntary, and you can withdraw them at any time. Withdrawing must be as easy as giving consent, and this applies to both consents. You withdraw the AI consent in the app’s settings or by contacting us. We then stop the processing and delete the stored health data, and the AI features can no longer be made available to you. At the same time, we delete the feedback and images you have sent us, the AI answers you have reported, and your ratings. You withdraw the consent for feedback in the feedback sheet, where you gave it, or by contacting us. At the bottom of the sheet, choose “Delete my feedback”; we then delete the feedback you have sent, including the images. Your food and symptom entries are not affected. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

5. Why we process your data, and our legal basis

  • Creating and operating your account, and delivering the subscription you have purchased – necessary for the performance of our contract with you, Article 6(1)(b).
  • Sending service messages, including welcome information, receipts, security notices and notices of changes to the terms – performance of the contract, Article 6(1)(b).
  • Storing and processing your health-related data and images so the AI features work – your explicit consent, Article 9(2)(a) together with Article 6(1)(a).
  • Sending our newsletter and other marketing – your consent, Article 6(1)(a), and section 15 of the Norwegian Marketing Control Act.
  • Security, troubleshooting, and preventing misuse, fraud and abuse of trial periods – our legitimate interest in a secure and functioning service, Article 6(1)(f).
  • Statistics and improvement of the app, based on aggregated and anonymised data – our legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f). Fully anonymised data falls outside the scope of the GDPR.
  • Following up bug reports and wishes you send in the app, and contacting you about the matter when you ask us to – your explicit consent, which you give when you send the feedback, Article 9(2)(a) together with Article 6(1)(a). The text is translated automatically only if you tick the box for it; see section 6.
  • Replying to emails and support enquiries, and receiving ratings you give in the app – our legitimate interest in replying to you and improving the app, Article 6(1)(f).
  • Reviewing AI answers you report in the chat, and correcting errors in the AI answers – our legitimate interest in correcting errors and improving the AI features, Article 6(1)(f).
  • Measuring which of our ads in the App Store (Apple Ads) lead to downloads and subscriptions – our legitimate interest in knowing what our marketing achieves, Article 6(1)(f).
  • Following up subscription revenue (the record of renewals), also after an account has been deleted – our legitimate interest in having an overview of our revenue, Article 6(1)(f).
  • Accounting and complying with statutory obligations – legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c).

6. How your data is processed by AI

The AI features – AI chat, the AI analysis and scanning of menus, buffets, dishes, barcodes and fridges – and the automatic translation of feedback, when you ask for it, are powered by AI models from two providers:
  • Gemini models from Google, via Google Cloud Vertex AI: scanning and the AI analysis from app version 2.1.0
  • Claude models from Anthropic: the AI chat, and scanning and the AI analysis in older versions of the app and as a fallback when the Google service is switched off or does not respond; in addition, when you correct or edit a scan and ask the AI to assess it again, and for the translation of feedback
The requests go from the app via our own server functions in Google Firebase to the provider. Our app development platform (FlutterFlow) is not part of this chain and does not receive your content.

What is sent

  • The text you write in the chat
  • In the chat: a limited amount of health context from your account, so that the answer fits your situation: your answers in “Your setup”, the summary of your most recent AI analysis, your five most recent symptom logs (date, complaints, stress and symptoms) and up to seven meals from your diary in the past week
  • For the AI analysis: your entries from the last 14 or 30 days, as you choose: the time and name of your meals, the foods with moderate or high FODMAP content that the app has found in them, your symptom logs (time, complaints, stress and symptoms) and your bowel movement logs. Notes, medication, sleep and menstruation are not sent
  • Photographs you submit for analysis, and ingredient lists retrieved for a scanned barcode
  • Names of individual ingredients that are not in our food database are looked up with Google (Gemini) to classify their FODMAP content. Only the ingredient name is sent, with nothing that links it to you
  • When you correct or edit a scan and ask the AI to assess it again: the name of the dish or food, the ingredient list with the levels you have set, and the previous assessment. The image is not sent
  • For feedback: if you use the app in a language other than Norwegian, you can tick “Automatically translate my text with AI (Claude)” when you send. The box is not ticked in advance. Only if you tick it is the text of your feedback sent to Claude, which translates it into Norwegian, and any greeting from us is then also translated into your language when the bug has been fixed or your wish has been fulfilled. Without the tick, neither the feedback nor the greeting is sent to the AI provider. The image is not sent
  • We do not send your name, email address, user ID or payment information
  • In an ongoing chat, the earlier messages in the same conversation are sent along, so that the AI can follow the thread. Your stored chat history from earlier sessions is not sent – each session starts a new conversation

Why, and on what basis

  • The sole purpose is to generate the answer or analysis you have asked for, or to translate feedback as described above
  • Because the content contains health data, the basis is your explicit consent, Article 9(2)(a) together with Article 6(1)(a). You give it before the AI features are made available to you. For the translation of feedback, you give it by ticking the box in the sheet when you send
  • Google and Anthropic both act as our data processors and process the content on our instructions, under Google’s data processing terms for Google Cloud and Anthropic’s data processing agreement. Google also runs the server functions that relay the requests
  • We do not use your content to train our own AI models, and we do not sell it. Neither provider uses it to train its AI models

Storage and deletion

  • Images you submit for analysis are stored temporarily in our cloud storage while the analysis is running, and are deleted automatically within one hour
  • Chat messages are deleted automatically after 24 hours – both by the app and by a server routine that runs every hour. You can also delete the conversation history yourself at any time in the app
  • An AI answer you report in the chat is kept beyond the 24 hours, until you delete your data, withdraw your consent or delete your account in the app; see section 10
  • Your food and symptom entries remain on your account until you delete them, withdraw your consent or delete your account. All of this can be done in the app
  • Google may cache requests and responses in memory, not on disk, for up to 24 hours to provide faster answers, and may log requests for a limited period to detect abuse of the service. Anthropic may retain content for a short period for operational and abuse-monitoring purposes under its own terms. The content is then deleted
  • A limited number of authorised personnel may access content where strictly necessary for troubleshooting, security or handling misuse

Important limitations

  • AI-generated answers may be incomplete or incorrect, and are general information, not medical advice
  • The AI makes no decision that has legal effect for you; see section 14
  • You decide what you write. We encourage you not to include more health information than is necessary to get a useful answer

7. Emails and marketing

Service messages

For as long as you hold an account, we send you the emails and notifications needed to deliver the service. These include a welcome email with getting-started information when you become a paying subscriber, password and security notifications, operational notices, and notices of changes to the terms or this privacy policy. Receipts for purchases are issued by Apple or Google. The basis for these is the performance of our contract with you, Article 6(1)(b), and you cannot unsubscribe from them while you hold an account.

We do not send our own reminder before a free trial ends. Apple and Google notify you themselves before a trial converts into a paid subscription, and you can cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play account.

Newsletter and marketing

We send our newsletter with low-FODMAP tips, recipes, news and offers only if you switch it on under «Newsletter by email» in the app’s settings. It is off by default, it is separate from your acceptance of the terms, it is voluntary, and it is not a condition for using FODMAPP.

You can switch it off again at any time in the same place, or use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. We record when consent is given and withdrawn so that we can demonstrate compliance with the rules on electronic marketing.

We do not sell or disclose your email address to others for marketing purposes, and we do not use your health data to target marketing.

8. Who we share your data with

We do not sell personal data. We use the following data processors and providers, all of whom are bound by a data processing agreement or equivalent terms:
  • Google Firebase – authentication, database, storage, server functions, abuse protection (App Check), crash reporting, performance monitoring and analytics
  • Google (Google Cloud Vertex AI) – the AI models (Gemini) that process text and images for scanning and in the AI analysis from app version 2.1.0, and lookups of ingredient names
  • Anthropic – the AI models (Claude) that process text and images in the AI chat, for scanning and in the AI analysis in older versions of the app and as a fallback, when a scan is assessed again and for the translation of feedback when you ask for it
  • RevenueCat – subscription management, using a pseudonymous subscriber ID, and measurement of our ads in the App Store (Apple Ads); see section 3(b)
  • Apple and Google – as sellers of subscriptions and providers of the app stores. Apple also reports which of our ads in the App Store led to a download (Apple Ads)
  • Resend – delivery of service emails and newsletters
  • Open Food Facts – product lookups by barcode. Only the barcode is sent; no personal data is transferred
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass – the toilet finder. Your approximate location is sent in order to look up nearby toilets; it is not stored and is not linked to your account
  • Content delivery networks – the app loads animations (LottieFiles), typefaces (Google Fonts) and certain images from external content networks. Your IP address is transmitted when that content loads; no account data is shared
We may also disclose data where we are required to do so by law, or in order to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

9. Transfers outside the EEA

Our database is located in the EU (Firestore, European multi-region), and your account data and your entries are stored there.

Some processing nevertheless takes place outside the EEA. The AI processing at Google is not restricted to a particular region and may take place outside the EEA, and Anthropic processes data in the United States. The server functions that relay the AI requests run mainly on infrastructure in the United States, as do some of our other server functions. Images you submit for analysis, and images you attach to feedback, are stored in our cloud storage at Google Firebase in the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA, we rely on the safeguards required by Chapter V of the GDPR: the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, and/or the provider’s certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

You may contact us at post@fodmapp.com for information about the safeguards that apply to a specific transfer.

10. How long we keep your data

  • Account data – for as long as you have an account. On deletion, data is removed within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days.
  • AI chat conversation history – deleted automatically after 24 hours. You can also delete it yourself at any time in the app.
  • Food and symptom entries – until you delete them, withdraw your consent, or delete your account.
  • Images for analysis – deleted automatically within one hour of upload.
  • Crash reports – up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics in Google Analytics – up to 2 months at user level. Aggregated reports, which cannot identify you, are kept longer.
  • Usage counters on your account – for as long as you have an account.
  • Records of rating prompts (when we have asked you for a rating, what you answered, and when the app has requested the store’s own rating dialogue) – for as long as you have an account, like the usage counters.
  • Purchase and accounting records – 5 years after the end of the financial year, as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act.
  • The record of subscription renewals (month, plan, country and store, linked to your user ID, not to your name or email address, without health data) – used to follow up subscription revenue and kept for 13 months after each renewal, also if the account is deleted in the meantime.
  • Subscription status (from RevenueCat) – for as long as the subscription runs. A subscription runs until you cancel it in the App Store or Google Play, also if you delete your account; the status then stays with us until the subscription has expired.
  • Record of consent to marketing – for as long as the consent is in force; it is stored with your account data and deleted with the account.
  • Feedback you send in the app (bug reports and wishes), with images – deleted automatically after 2 years, and earlier if you choose “Delete my feedback” in the feedback sheet, delete your data, withdraw the AI consent or delete your account in the app. In addition, we keep a separate list of the times of your submissions in order to limit them to 5 per day. It is used only for that and is deleted automatically by a nightly routine, no later than three days after your last submission.
  • Support correspondence by email – up to 2 years.
  • AI answers you report, bug reports from older versions of the app, and app ratings – until you delete your data, withdraw the AI consent or delete your account in the app.
Whatever is deleted, whether you delete it yourself or it is deleted automatically, also disappears from our backups within 90 days, the same deadline as for the account.

11. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all communication between the app, our servers and our providers. Passwords are stored hashed and are never available to us in plain text. Access to production data is restricted to the small number of people who need it, and is protected by two-factor authentication.

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Norwegian Data Protection Authority within 72 hours, and we will notify you where the law requires it.

12. Your rights

You have the right to:
  • Access – be told what data we hold about you and receive a copy
  • Rectification – have incorrect data corrected
  • Erasure – have your data deleted, including the right to be forgotten
  • Restriction – ask us to limit our processing
  • Object – object to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • Data portability – receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent – at any time, without giving a reason
  • Lodge a complaint – with a supervisory authority
To exercise your rights, contact post@fodmapp.com. We reply without undue delay and no later than one month. See also our Delete account page.

If you believe we are processing your personal data unlawfully, you may complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), postboks 458 Sentrum, 0105 Oslo, www.datatilsynet.no. If you live in another EEA country, you may also complain to the supervisory authority in your own country.

13. Children

FODMAPP is not intended for children. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account and use the app. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has nevertheless provided us with personal data, please contact us at post@fodmapp.com and we will delete the information.

14. Automated decision-making and profiling

The AI features generate information, assessments and recommendations, but they do not make any decision that has legal effect for you or otherwise significantly affects you within the meaning of Article 22 of the GDPR. You always decide for yourself what you eat.

We do not carry out profiling for advertising purposes, we do not use your health data to build marketing profiles, and we do not disclose data to advertising networks. When we measure our ads in the App Store (section 3(b)), only the attribution token from Apple is sent back to Apple, so that Apple can report which ad led to the download.

15. Cookies and the website

Our website uses only what is necessary to make the site work. We store your choice of language and your response to the cookie banner locally in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts and certain images and store badges from external sources (including Apple and Wikimedia), which means your IP address is transmitted to those providers when a page loads. The app itself uses Firebase Analytics as described in section 3(e).

16. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy, for example when we introduce new features or when the rules change. The date and version number are shown at the top of the page.

In the case of material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email in good time, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent afresh. Older versions are available on request.
