Privacy Policy

Here we explain how we process personal data when you use the FODMAPP app.

Last updated: April 10, 2026

1. About the app

FODMAPP is developed to help users navigate the FODMAP diet and get guidance on foods, recipes, and symptom relief. The app uses artificial intelligence (AI) to provide personalized information and uses image analysis to recognize foods, menus, and ingredient lists.

The app uses Firebase (Google) for secure data storage. AI features are powered by Gemini (Google AI).

2. What data we collect

The app collects a minimum of data to function as intended. This may include:

a) User data

  • Email address or login method – email/password, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In (if you create an account)
  • Language setting and app version
  • User consent to terms and privacy

b) Image and camera data

  • Images you take or upload for analysis (foods, menus, barcodes, fridge contents, etc.)

➜ These images are temporarily stored in Firebase and automatically deleted after use.

➜ Images are only used for real-time analysis purposes by the AI system (Gemini).

c) AI interactions

  • Text you write in the AI chat (for questions about food, symptoms, recipes, etc.)

➜ Text is temporarily sent to Gemini for processing and response.

➜ We do not store conversation logs locally or share them.

d) Technical data (anonymous)

  • Device information (model, operating system, app version)
  • Error reports (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • App usage statistics (via Firebase Analytics, anonymized)

3. How we use the information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver core app features (AI chat and AI camera)
  • Improve user experience and reliability
  • Ensure technical operation and detect errors
  • Customize content by language and preferences

We do not use personal data for marketing, profiling, or third-party advertising.

4. Sharing of data

We do not share personal data with third parties, except:

  • Google Firebase: For secure temporary storage of images and technical data
  • Google Gemini AI: For real-time processing of text and images
  • Open Food Facts: For barcode product lookups (ingredient data only, no personal data)
  • RevenueCat: For subscription management (anonymous user ID only)

All services comply with EU GDPR and have strict data security requirements.

5. Storage and deletion

  • Images are automatically deleted from Firebase shortly after analysis (usually within 24 hours).
  • Login data is retained as long as you have an active account.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated information at any time.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure all communication between app, server, and AI services. Data access is limited to systems and developers with necessary authorization.

7. Your rights

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Access what data is stored about you
  • Request correction or deletion of data
  • Withdraw consent to data processing
  • File a complaint with the Data Protection Authority if you believe personal data is handled in violation of the law

For privacy questions, contact us at: post@fodmapp.com

8. Changes to the privacy policy

We may update this policy as needed, for example due to changes in technology or regulations. When this happens, you will be notified in the app and can read the updated version before continued use.
