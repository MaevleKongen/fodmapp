Privacy Policy

Here we explain how we process personal data when you use the FODMAPP app.

Last updated: 13 August 2026 · Version 2.0

1. About this privacy policy

This privacy policy explains how Telemark Software Solutions AS processes personal data when you use the FODMAPP app and our website. It applies to everyone who uses FODMAPP.

Telemark Software Solutions AS is the data controller for the processing described here. You can contact us at post@fodmapp.com.

FODMAPP is built on Google Firebase for authentication, storage and operations, and uses Google’s Gemini AI models for the AI features. Because the app deals with food, digestion and symptoms, we process health data, and we therefore explain in particular detail below what happens to that information.

2. In brief

  • We process information about your diet, digestion and symptoms. This is health data, and we process it only on the basis of your explicit consent.
  • Your AI conversations and the food and symptom entries you record are stored on your account so that you have a history and can get more relevant answers. You can have them deleted.
  • Text and images you send to the AI features are transferred to Google’s Gemini models for processing. We do not use your data to train our own AI models.
  • Photographs are stored temporarily and deleted within 24 hours.
  • We send you necessary service emails. Our newsletter is sent only if you have separately consented to it, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • We never sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or third-party profiling.
  • You can request access, correction and deletion at any time, and withdraw your consent.

3. What personal data we collect

a) Account data

  • Email address, and first and last name if you provide them
  • Password, stored in encrypted (hashed) form – or your login method if you use Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, in which case we receive your name and email address from them
  • Language setting, app version and country/region
  • Record of the consents you have given: which version of the terms and privacy policy you accepted, when, and whether you have consented to marketing

b) Subscription data

  • Subscription status and type, trial period status, start and renewal dates
  • A pseudonymous subscriber ID with RevenueCat, and the purchase history reported by the app store
  • We never receive or store your card details. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google

c) Health-related data

  • The messages you write in the AI chat, including anything you tell us about digestion, symptoms, reactions to food, diagnoses or medication
  • Food and symptom entries you record in the app
  • The AI’s analyses, FODMAP scores and recommendations linked to your account
  • The history of the above, which is stored on your account until you delete it or delete your account

d) Images

  • Photographs you take or upload for analysis – food, dishes, menus, ingredient lists, barcodes or the contents of your fridge
  • Images are stored temporarily for the analysis and are deleted automatically within 24 hours

e) Technical data

  • Device model, operating system, app version and language
  • Crash and error reports via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Usage statistics via Firebase Analytics, such as which features are used and how often
  • This data is pseudonymous: it is not linked to your name, but it is linked to an app-instance identifier and is therefore still personal data

f) Correspondence

  • Emails and support enquiries you send us, and our replies

4. Health data and your explicit consent

Information about digestion, symptoms and reactions to food is classified as data concerning health, which is a special category of personal data under Article 9 of the GDPR. Such data may only be processed where a specific condition is met.

We process your health data solely on the basis of your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a), together with Article 6(1)(a). You give this consent in a separate dialogue in the app before the AI features are made available to you, and it covers the storage of your conversations and entries and the transfer of the content to our AI provider for processing.

The consent is voluntary. You may withdraw it at any time in the app’s settings or by contacting us, and withdrawal is as simple as giving consent. If you withdraw your consent, we stop the processing and delete the stored health data, and the AI features can no longer be made available to you. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

5. Why we process your data, and our legal basis

  • Creating and operating your account, and delivering the subscription you have purchased – necessary for the performance of our contract with you, Article 6(1)(b).
  • Storing and processing your health-related data and images so the AI features work – your explicit consent, Article 9(2)(a) together with Article 6(1)(a).
  • Sending service messages, including welcome information, trial-period and payment reminders, receipts, security notices and notices of changes to the terms – performance of the contract, Article 6(1)(b).
  • Sending our newsletter and other marketing – your consent, Article 6(1)(a), and section 15 of the Norwegian Marketing Control Act.
  • Security, troubleshooting, and preventing misuse, fraud and abuse of trial periods – our legitimate interest in a secure and functioning service, Article 6(1)(f).
  • Statistics and improvement of the app, based on aggregated and anonymised data – our legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f). Fully anonymised data falls outside the scope of the GDPR.
  • Accounting and complying with statutory obligations – legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c).

6. How your data is processed by AI

The AI features – AI chat, camera analysis and barcode scanning – are powered by Google’s Gemini models. The AI integration is delivered through our app platform (FlutterFlow) and Google’s infrastructure.

What is sent

  • The text you write in the chat, including any health information you choose to include
  • Photographs you submit for analysis
  • Ingredient lists retrieved for a scanned barcode
  • Limited context needed for a sensible reply, such as your language and relevant earlier messages in the same conversation
  • We do not send your name, email address or payment information to the AI provider

Why, and on what basis

  • The sole purpose is to generate the answer or analysis you have asked for
  • Because the content may contain health data, the basis is your explicit consent, Article 9(2)(a) together with Article 6(1)(a)
  • The AI provider acts as our data processor and processes the content on our instructions
  • We do not use your content to train our own AI models, and we do not sell it

Storage and deletion

  • Images are deleted from our storage automatically within 24 hours
  • Your conversation history and your food and symptom entries remain on your account until you delete them, withdraw your consent or delete your account
  • The AI provider may retain content for a short period for operational and abuse-monitoring purposes under its own terms, after which it is deleted
  • A limited number of authorised personnel may access content where strictly necessary for troubleshooting, security or handling misuse

Important limitations

  • AI-generated answers may be incomplete or incorrect, and are general information, not medical advice
  • The AI makes no decision that has legal effect for you; see section 14
  • You decide what you write. We encourage you not to include more health information than is necessary to get a useful answer

7. Emails and marketing

Service messages

For as long as you hold an account, we send you the emails and notifications necessary to deliver the service: a welcome email and getting-started information, confirmation of your consents, a reminder before a free trial period ends and payment is charged, receipts and information about subscription changes, password and security notices, operational notices and notices of changes to the terms or this privacy policy. The basis for these is the performance of our contract with you, Article 6(1)(b), and you cannot unsubscribe from them while you hold an account.

Newsletter and marketing

We send our newsletter with low-FODMAP tips, recipes, news and offers only if you have given your consent. That consent is given separately from your acceptance of the terms, it is voluntary, and it is not a condition for using FODMAPP.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or by changing the setting in the app. We keep a record of consents given and withdrawn in order to document compliance with the rules on electronic marketing.

We do not sell or disclose your email address to others for marketing purposes, and we do not use your health data to target marketing.

8. Who we share your data with

We do not sell personal data. We use the following data processors and providers, all of whom are bound by a data processing agreement or equivalent terms:
  • Google Firebase – authentication, database, storage, crash reporting and analytics
  • Google Gemini / Google Cloud – processing of text and images in the AI features
  • FlutterFlow – the app platform through which the AI integration is delivered
  • RevenueCat – subscription management, using a pseudonymous subscriber ID
  • Apple and Google – as sellers of subscriptions and providers of the app stores
  • Our email provider – sending service messages and newsletters
  • Open Food Facts – product lookups by barcode. Only the barcode is sent; no personal data is transferred
We may also disclose data where we are required to do so by law, or in order to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

9. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of our providers are established outside the EEA, or process data on infrastructure located outside the EEA, including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA, we rely on the safeguards required by Chapter V of the GDPR: the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, and/or the provider’s certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Where our providers make European regions available, we seek to have data stored and processed within the EU/EEA. You may contact us at post@fodmapp.com for information about the safeguards that apply to a specific transfer.

10. How long we keep your data

  • Account data – for as long as you have an account. On deletion, data is removed within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days.
  • Conversation history, and food and symptom entries – until you delete them, withdraw your consent, or delete your account.
  • Images – a maximum of 24 hours after the analysis.
  • Crash reports – up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics – up to 14 months, in aggregated form.
  • Purchase and accounting records – 5 years after the end of the financial year, as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act.
  • Record of consent to marketing – for as long as the consent is in force, and for up to 3 years after it is withdrawn, as documentation.
  • Support correspondence – up to 2 years.

11. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all communication between the app, our servers and our providers. Passwords are stored hashed and are never available to us in plain text. Access to production data is restricted to the small number of people who need it, and is protected by two-factor authentication.

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Norwegian Data Protection Authority within 72 hours, and we will notify you where the law requires it.

12. Your rights

You have the right to:
  • Access – be told what data we hold about you and receive a copy
  • Rectification – have incorrect data corrected
  • Erasure – have your data deleted, including the right to be forgotten
  • Restriction – ask us to limit our processing
  • Object – object to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • Data portability – receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent – at any time, without giving a reason
  • Lodge a complaint – with a supervisory authority
To exercise your rights, contact post@fodmapp.com. We reply without undue delay and no later than one month. See also our Delete account page.

If you believe we are processing your personal data unlawfully, you may complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), postboks 458 Sentrum, 0105 Oslo, www.datatilsynet.no. If you live in another EEA country, you may also complain to the supervisory authority in your own country.

13. Children

FODMAPP is not intended for children. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account and use the app. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has nevertheless provided us with personal data, please contact us at post@fodmapp.com and we will delete the information.

14. Automated decision-making and profiling

The AI features generate information, assessments and recommendations, but they do not make any decision that has legal effect for you or otherwise significantly affects you within the meaning of Article 22 of the GDPR. You always decide for yourself what you eat.

We do not carry out profiling for advertising purposes, we do not use your health data to build marketing profiles, and we do not disclose data to advertising networks.

15. Cookies and the website

Our website uses only what is necessary to make the site work. We store your choice of language and your response to the cookie banner locally in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is transmitted to Google when a page loads. The app itself uses Firebase Analytics as described in section 3(e).

16. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy, for example when we introduce new features or when the rules change. The date and version number are shown at the top of the page.

In the case of material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email in good time, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent afresh. Older versions are available on request.
