FODMAPP Manual

FODMAPP helps you scan your food, log your gut and see what keeps coming up. Here you'll find everything the app can do and how to use it.

ScanBarcodeLoggingAnalysis and reportAI chatToilet mapSettingsTips
📸 AI Camera

Everything you can photograph

Point the camera at the dish, the menu, the buffet or the fridge, and see the FODMAP level of every ingredient, dish and product.

🍲 Dish

Snap your plate. The food scanner recognizes the ingredients, estimates the amounts and shows which are low, moderate and high in FODMAP, with swap ideas for a lower level.

📋 Restaurant menu

Snap a menu in any language. The dishes appear in your language with their FODMAP level, and under “To the waiter” you get a ready-made note in the menu's language to show.

🥗 Hot and cold buffets

Take one photo of the whole buffet table. The AI finds the dishes and shows which are low, moderate and high in FODMAP, so you get the full picture fast.

🧊 Fridge

Snap your fridge. FODMAPP finds the foods, and you can add anything the AI missed. Then you get recipes made only from the low-FODMAP foods, scaled to the number of servings.

🔢 Levels and FODMAP groups

Every scan shows the level with a color and three bars: low, moderate or high. You also see the amount and which FODMAP group sets the level, such as fructans.

✏️ Edit and reassess

Something off? Tap “Edit”, add or remove ingredients, change the amounts and ask the AI to reassess.

🍽️ Log what you ate

Tap “Log as eaten” once you've eaten what you scanned. You choose the portion afterwards, and the meal goes straight into your diary.

🗂️ My scans

All your scans are gathered in one place, with search. Filter by dish, menu, buffet, fridge or barcode, and open an earlier result again.

📏 Amounts in your units

Amounts are shown in metric or US units, based on your country.

🏷️ Barcode Scanner

Instant product checks in the store

Point the camera at any product's barcode and get a full FODMAP assessment in seconds.

🌍 Over 4.4 million products

The barcode scanner taps into Open Food Facts – one of the world's largest open food databases with over 4.4 million products from countries around the globe. Whether you're at home or travelling abroad, chances are the product is already in the database.

📊 AI-powered ingredient analysis

When you scan the barcode, the app fetches the ingredient list on the pack and assesses every ingredient, with serving size and lower-FODMAP alternatives.

✅ Shopping made easier

Use the scanner before putting anything in your trolley. It works with packaged products from any country – perfect when travelling or shopping at international stores.

📝 Logging

Log your gut in two minutes

Food diary and symptom diary in one. The more often you log, the more the app can show you.

😣 Symptom log

Record pain and stress from 0 to 10, symptoms like bloating and cramps, sleep, medication and menstruation. You can add your own symptoms and a note.

✅ No symptoms today

Save a good day with one tap. It counts as a logged day in the diary and the analysis.

📋 Copy previous log

Is today like last time? Copy your previous log and adjust what's different.

🚽 Bathroom visits

Log bathroom visits with the Bristol scale from 1 to 7, and add details like urgency if you want.

🍽️ Meals

Type in what you've eaten, or log a scan as eaten.

🔔 Reminder

Pick a time, and the app reminds you to log.

✨ AI Analysis and report

See what keeps coming up

The Pain diary, AI Analysis and the report are based on what you've logged.

📔 Pain diary

See your days by week, month or year, with average pain, a color-coded calendar, a chart for pain and stress, and which symptoms keep coming back.

✨ New AI analysis

Choose the last 14 or 30 days. Once you've logged at least 10 days in the period, you get a summary of your logs. You can run three analyses per day.

📊 Most often before symptoms

The app itself counts which foods with moderate or high FODMAP content you ate in the 24 hours before logged symptoms, ranked and with the number of times.

📈 Pain and stress

A chart shows the highest pain and stress per day in the period, side by side.

💬 Ask the chat about the analysis

Go through the results with the AI chat, and read the answers to common questions about how the numbers are counted.

🗂️ Previous analyses

Your analyses are saved, so you can open an older analysis again.

📄 Report for your doctor

Choose the period yourself and share a PDF with symptoms, bowel movements, meals and medication with your doctor or dietitian.

💬 AI Chat

What can you ask the AI?

Type a question and get an answer in seconds – in your own language, 24/7.

🥦 Evaluate any food

Type a food, a dish or a product name and get a FODMAP assessment with an explanation, portion advice and low-FODMAP alternatives.

🍽️ Analyse restaurant dishes

Describe a restaurant dish, and the AI assesses each ingredient, explains the FODMAP content and suggests how to order a low-FODMAP version.

📅 Weekly meal plans

Ask for a full low-FODMAP weekly menu with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks – the AI creates a varied and balanced plan with recipes.

🛒 Shopping lists

After your meal plan, ask for a shopping list. The AI generates one based on the menu so you always have the right ingredients at home.

❓ FODMAP questions

Ask what FODMAP is, which types exist (fructans, galactans, polyols, fructose, lactose), how the diet works, or anything else related to FODMAP.

🧘 Stress management

Ask the AI for relaxation techniques, breathing exercises and practical tips for handling stress in everyday life.

💡 Suggestions for you

The chat suggests questions based on your scans and meals, such as low-FODMAP alternatives to something you've scanned or a recipe for something you've eaten.

🙋 Knows you from the start

The answers you give when you start using the app are used as background in the chat, so you don't have to repeat yourself. You can change them anytime.

🕒 Deleted after 24 hours

Messages stay in the chat for 24 hours and are then deleted automatically.

🚻 Toilet map

Find a toilet nearby

Toilet data from OpenStreetMap, maps from Google.

📍 Near you

The map and the list show toilets around you, with distances.

🔎 Search the area

Move the map and search for toilets in the area you're viewing.

🎛️ Filters

Filter by free, wheelchair access, changing table and open now.

🧭 Directions

Get directions to the toilet with one tap.

⚙️ Settings

Make the app yours

You'll find language, setup, data and subscription under Settings in the app.

🌍 20+ languages

Use the app in your language. The scans, the analysis and the chat answer in the language you've chosen.

🧭 Your setup

You can change the answers you gave when you got started, such as your gut type and the symptoms you already knew about, anytime.

💬 Feedback

Found a bug or have a wish? Go to Settings, find “Feedback” and tap “Report a problem or send a wish”. When what you reported is in place, you'll be notified in the app.

🔒 Your data

You can export or delete all your data whenever you like, right from the app.

⭐ FODMAPP Plus

Everything is unlocked for the first 14 days. After that, FODMAPP Plus keeps every feature open. The subscription renews automatically and can be canceled in the App Store or Google Play.

✦ Tips

Good to know

📸 Take clear photos

Make sure there's good lighting and the entire food item or menu is visible in the camera frame for the best analysis.

📶 Internet connection

The scans, the analysis and the chat need internet. Wi-Fi gives the fastest answers.

⭐ Build routines

Use the app whenever you're in doubt – at home, at the supermarket, or at a restaurant. The more you use it, the more confident you'll become.

🔒 Privacy first

Photos you take for analysis are stored only temporarily while the AI analyses them, and are deleted automatically within one hour. Your AI chat conversations are deleted automatically after 24 hours. Your food and symptom entries are stored on your account so you can look back at them, and you can delete them in the app whenever you want.

📏 Portion size matters

FODMAP content depends on how much you eat. Try to include the portion size when chatting with the AI – it makes the answer more accurate.

🗓️ Use the app in everyday life

The low-FODMAP diet is often followed in consultation with a doctor or dietitian. FODMAPP helps you scan your food, log your gut and see what keeps coming up.
